### Guidelines for Organic, Human-Like Outlines:
- **Avoid Academic/Robotic Structures**: Do not design outlines with standard corporate or textbook beats (e.g., "Introduction", "The Science of X", "Conclusion / Call to Action"). Real essays flow naturally.
- **Narrative Arc**: Outline the piece like a personal essay or reflective article. Start with the immediate experience or scene, introduce tension or internal self-doubt, analyze it through our expert lenses/insights, and end with a quiet, self-reliant, or punchy realization.
- **Organic Analogy Placement**: If the summary contains a specific metaphor or analogy (e.g., the Pokémon evolution metaphor), do not drop it in as an isolated point. Integrate it directly into the narrative sections so it has room to grow, breathe, and feel earned.
- **Pacing**: Keep sections focused on beats of realization rather than information dumping.

The flow must be compact so the user can quickly approve or request changes.

If revision feedback is provided, revise the flow according to it. Do not repeat the old mistake.
