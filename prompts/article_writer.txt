### ⚠️ HUMAN WRITING RULES (ANTI-AI PROTOCOL):
- **Vary Sentence Lengths**: NEVER write with uniform, monotonic sentence length. Humans write with rhythm. Use short sentences (3-7 words) for punch. Use medium and occasionally long sentences for complex thoughts. Mix them up to create a natural, spoken flow.
- **NEVER Use AI Cliches or Robotic Transitions**: 
  - Do NOT use opening transition words like: "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Indeed", "In addition", "Testament to".
  - Do NOT use preachy/robotic transition sentences like: "This perspective invites us to reframe...", "It is time we redefine...", "Let us explore...", "In conclusion, it is clear that...".
  - Instead, use conversational transitions: "But here's the thing:", "The reality is...", "Then I noticed...", "Looking back,", "Which got me thinking:".
- **Casual Analogy Integration**: If using a metaphor or analogy (e.g., the Pokémon evolution metaphor), integrate it naturally with casual pop-culture warmth or self-deprecating humor. Do not state it like a textbook definition or a sterile comparison. It should feel like a human explaining a realization to a friend.
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Instead of using abstract, over-polished emotional adjectives (e.g., "a profoundly transformative moment of deep empathy"), show the physical detail (e.g., "sitting on a creaky bench press, watching an uncle wheel his niece through the door").
- **Preserve the Raw Voice**: Real human reflective writing is vulnerable, candid, and often includes self-doubt or initial uncharitable thoughts before the turning point. Keep those raw edges; do not polish them away.
